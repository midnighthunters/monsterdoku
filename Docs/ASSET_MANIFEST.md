## Procedural UI and VFX

- Rounded nine-sliced panel/button sprites are generated by `RuntimeArt.RoundedSprite`; they are deterministic code-native art.
- Back, settings, theme, hint, undo, restart, lock, completion, X, play, level-state, and accessibility treatments are typography-plus-rounded-shape UI primitives. No downloaded icon pack is used.
- Ten chapter ambiences reuse original layered moon/circle, star, colour, and panel pieces with distinct palette context rather than shipping large decorative backdrops.
- Monster expressions are lightweight presentation states: neutral, hint/highlight pulse, mistake shake, placement settle, and sequential celebration pop.

## Typography

TextMeshPro Essential Resources are imported from Unity's installed `com.unity.ugui` package. The bundled Liberation Sans SDF font is used under Unity's package licence. No external font download is required.

## Audio

The Unity MCP audio provider was inspected but no provider key was configured. All shipped audio is therefore original deterministic synthesis in `AudioService`:

- tap, X, monster placement, mistake/heart loss, hint, panel, and victory tones;
- seamless eight-second light and dark harmonic ambience loops.

No copied samples or external audio files are included.
